F18: finishing the share inside the share sheet (flow mock)

One column per option and one row per moment. A is the Latchkey icon in the share sheet's app row. B and C are the "Send to Latchkey" Shortcut in the share sheet's actions list: only an App Intent runs where the tailnet is, and the app-row extension can neither reach the gateway nor open Latchkey (spec §2). Example content: sharing a Safari article to the obsidian session on chonk.

A: queued from the app-row sheetPick in the sheet. Sent when you next open Latchkey. No switch, no confirmation. B: Shortcut, Latchkey comes forwardPick in the Shortcut prompt. Latchkey opens and sends. Confirmed, with a switch. C: Shortcut, sent in the backgroundPick in the Shortcut prompt. Sent from the background. Confirmed, no switch, if it fits iOS's time budget.

Reading the columns. A is the only option that uses the Latchkey icon, and it can never say "Sent" in the sheet. B always confirms, at the cost of the switch. C is the only way to get both, and only from the Shortcut. It rests on two things nobody has measured: how long iOS lets a background intent run, and whether the dashboard page works while the app is backgrounded. A can be built alongside B or C.
