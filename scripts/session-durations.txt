# launch and the sign-in again — scripts/shards.py keeps each group together.

# which is why they measure about 0.1 s.
#
# These figures are from an eight-way run, where test time runs about 1.39x
# its serial cost because the suite drives the real KiroCrew bundle in WebKit.
