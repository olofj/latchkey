## 2. Auth client, 0.7.1 vs 0.6.0

Unchanged:
- **403 detection:** status 403 with header `X-Auth-Required: true`. 0.7.1 also registers the handler as a hook (`f(V)`), with the same condition.
- **Refresh call:** `fetch('/api/auth/refresh', {method:'POST', credentials:'include'})`, no body, one call in flight at a time.
- **Terminal errors:** in the client, 401 is terminal (no more silent refresh) and anything else is transient. In main, 401 with `body.error === 'refresh_chain_revoked'` is terminal and runs `onChainRevoked` or `location.assign('/')`. Other failures back off at 60s, 240s, 960s, then 3600s.
- **`/api/auth/me` on boot:** `GET` with `credentials:'include'`.
  - 404 means refresh is unsupported.
  - 401, or 403 with `X-Auth-Required`, triggers a silent refresh and one retry.
  - It reads `session_exp` (epoch seconds) and schedules a refresh 3600s before it, never sooner than 5s.
- **Banner:** id `mc-session-expired`, prepended to body. It fires `mc-auth-required` on show and `mc-auth-cleared` on clear or dismiss. Inside an iframe the page posts `{type:'mc-auth-expired'}` to its parent instead.

New in 0.7.1:
- **Token paste in the banner.** 0.6.0 redirected to `/?token=`. 0.7.1 instead calls `GET /api/auth/me?token=&lt;t&gt;` with `credentials:'include'`. It reads `token_accepted` and `owner_ok` (the stale-owner case needs both true), and on success clears the banner in place. The fake already returns both fields (`fake_gateway.py` ~780-787).
- **New event `mc-auth-recovered`:** fired together with `mc-auth-cleared` after a successful recovery. App listens for it; Latchkey does not.
