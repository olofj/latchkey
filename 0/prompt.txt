- **setUp** (SessionTests.swift:52-67, ShareTests.swift:35-48): about eight loopback requests with no waits. The 5 s timeouts only matter on failure. Negligible.
- **Launch plus sign-in** is the floor, about 12-13 s. The shortest signed-in tests (QR 13.8 s, chips 14.6 s) are almost all launch and sign-in. setUp's `__reset` ends every session, so a signed-in app can't be carried from one test to the next.
